    Questions

    Before you book

    Do I need to be home?

    No. For a mobile detail we only need the car unlocked or the keys left somewhere safe. For interiors we'll text you when we arrive and when we finish.

    Do you need my water or electricity?

    No. The van carries its own water tank and a quiet generator, so we never use your hose or outlets.

    What happens if it rains?

    Interior work goes ahead as planned. For exterior work we'll move you to the next dry slot at no charge, or work under a garage roof if you have one.

    How does pickup and drop-off work?

    A Lucky's driver collects the car from your home in the window you choose, details it at a covered partner bay, and returns it the same day. The car is covered by our insurance the whole time.

    Can you work in an apartment complex or office lot?

    Usually, yes. Some complexes don't allow washing on site. If yours doesn't, choose pickup and drop-off instead.

    How do I pay?

    Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or cash, after you've inspected the finished car. No deposit for standard packages. Showroom bookings take a $100 deposit.
